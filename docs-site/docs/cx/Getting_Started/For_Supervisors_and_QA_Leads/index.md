# Quick Start for Supervisors & QA Leads

Supervisors, Quality Managers, and Evaluators share responsibility for contact center performance and quality. Supervisors monitor live queues and agent availability in real time. Quality Managers design evaluation programs and oversee both human and AI-driven audits, while Evaluators review and score the individual interactions assigned to them.

Work through the guide for your role.

## Supervisor

You monitor live queues, track agent availability, and intervene in conversations when needed. Your primary tools are the real-time dashboards.

→ [Monitoring Your Team in Real Time](./Monitoring-Your-Team-in-Real-Time.md)

## Quality Manager

You design evaluation strategy, configure pass/fail thresholds, schedule audits, and oversee both human and AI-driven quality workflows.

→ [Managing the Quality Assurance Workflow](./Managing-the-Quality-Assurance-Workflow.md)

## Evaluator

You review conversations assigned to you, score them against a rubric, and submit evaluations before their deadlines.

→ [Evaluator Guide](./Evaluator-Guide.md)
